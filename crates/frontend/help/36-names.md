title: Other names

Many shows go by more than one title: a translated or romanised title, the
original one, a shortened or older one, or the name release groups use for
it. Single seasons can have names of their own too, such as an anime's
second cour. [XEM](remotes) collects these, each with its language, and
track lists the ones it has under the show's ![Names](button:tag) **Names**
action, titled **Other names**.

The list shows the names for the whole show first, then each season's.
XEM names seasons by TheTVDB or another numbering, so a season's names sit
under the season of that numbering they belong to, such as **TheTVDB
Season 2**; which seasons those are follows the show's [episode
numbering](numberings). The show's own titles are left out of its list.

## Where they come from

The names are fetched with the show's XEM mappings when it syncs, as long as
it has an XEM remote that is turned on. Shows XEM doesn't know, and shows it
knows no other names for, have no **Names** action. The list can't be edited
in track; a missing or wrong name is fixed on XEM's website.

## What track uses them for

The names are there for reference, to recognise a show under another title.
Syncing also uses them, beside the show's own titles, to find the show's
page on XEM's website for its XEM remote's link. Track's search doesn't look
at them.
